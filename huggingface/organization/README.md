# Namche

**Practical AI, informed by evidence.**

Namche combines AI consulting, implementation and product development in
Dornbirn, Austria. We work with organisations on concrete projects and develop
products of our own, with attention to human agency, European self-determination
and control over data and infrastructure.

On Hugging Face, we share datasets and evaluation results so others can inspect
our methods and build on the work.

## Propaganda Eval DE

[**803 questions, two languages, four models.**](https://huggingface.co/datasets/namcheai/propaganda-eval-de)
A German translation of Nous Research's Propaganda_Eval, with English originals,
translation and review prompts, documented review decisions and paired
English-German evaluations of Arete, Qwen, Kolibri and Apertus.

- [Dataset, review records and results](https://huggingface.co/datasets/namcheai/propaganda-eval-de)
- [Code, methods and prompts](https://github.com/NamcheAI/propaganda-eval-de)
- [Read the findings in English](https://namche.ai/insights/arete-auf-deutsch?language=en)
- [Ergebnisse auf Deutsch lesen](https://namche.ai/insights/arete-auf-deutsch?language=de)

The original benchmark and Arete model are by
[Nous Research](https://nousresearch.com/arete). Namche translated and evaluated
the benchmark; we did not train a model. Each release documents its sources,
model settings and limitations. These results describe behaviour under a
specific rubric, not general political neutrality.
